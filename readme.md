# Food Health Risk Analytics Dashboard

A data analytics project focused on evaluating the health risk of food items using nutritional information, feature engineering, SQL analysis, and interactive Power BI visualizations.

## Dashboard Preview

![Food Health Risk Analytics Dashboard](dashboard/dashboard-overview.png)

## Project Progress

### Completed

* Data understanding and exploratory analysis
* Data cleaning and preprocessing
* Feature engineering
* Health risk score calculation
* Risk category classification (Low, Moderate, High)
* SQL-based nutrition analysis
* Interactive Power BI dashboard development
* KPI creation and visualization
* Food risk distribution analysis
* Nutrition density vs health risk analysis
* Top healthiest foods identification
* Top highest-risk foods identification

### Dashboard Features

* Total Foods KPI
* Average Risk Score KPI
* Low Risk Percentage KPI
* Average Nutrition Density KPI
* Risk Category Distribution
* Health Risk Score vs Nutrition Density Scatter Plot
* Top 10 Healthiest Foods
* Top 10 Highest Risk Foods
* Interactive slicers for:

  * Risk Category
  * Food Item
  * Spice & Condiment Classification
  * Energy (kcal)
